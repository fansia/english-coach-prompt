02 · How it teaches

    
Like a tutor who reads your level

    
    
      
  1. Adjusts its speed
    When you miss more, it slows down on its own; when you do better, it speeds back up.
    2. 
      
  2. Makes you sound native
    When you stumble, it rephrases what you said more naturally and has you repeat the corrected sentence.
    3. 
      
  3. Breaks up long sentences
    If a sentence is too long to say in one go, it splits it into phrases and walks you through them one at a time.
    4. 
    

  


  

    
03 · One lesson a day

    
20 minutes, starting from a real, recent topic

    

      
ListenNo text yet
Each day it picks an everyday, recent topic (news, health, travel, food, sport, tech) and a short text at your level, reads it aloud, then asks what you heard.

      
UnderstandWords, phrases, context
Then it shows the text and explains the key words, phrases, structures and background.

      
SpeakConversation and correction
A natural conversation on the topic, correcting grammar, naturalness, vocabulary, fluency and pronunciation, separating mistakes from "understandable but unnatural" and more native phrasing.

      
ReviewBefore it ends
You say today's 2–5 key expressions out loud again, with instant correction, then finish with the one or two sentences most worth remembering.

    

    
It also tracks your weak spots and progress to shape later topics, difficulty and focus, and avoids repeating a topic.

  


  

    
04 · FAQ

    
Questions

    
Does it cost anything?
The prompt is free. Which features you get (voice, scheduled reminders) depends on your ChatGPT plan.

    
How do I make it a daily lesson?
After pasting the prompt, ask ChatGPT to set it up as a daily scheduled reminder. Want a different flow or level? Tell it during the lesson, then ask it to save the change.

    
Do I have to use my phone?
No, but voice practice is smoothest in the ChatGPT phone app.

    
Is it all in English?
Yes. The whole lesson is in English unless you ask for a Chinese explanation.
